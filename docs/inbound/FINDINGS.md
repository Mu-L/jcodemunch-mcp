# FINDINGS — what the inbound layer needed and did not have (2026-09-04)

What the harness, the CI gates, the workflow layer, the product, or the
repository's settings would have to expose for the jobs in `DESIGN.md` to
do their work, and what this layer could not change itself. Rows are
appended, never rewritten; a fix changes the status column only.

| ID | Finding | Where | Remedy | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
